# Vana Path — Grove of Ash

A short 3D action RPG built around the existing jungle and mandapa level.

## Play

From `game/`:

```powershell
npm run dev
```

Open `http://localhost:3000`.

- Move: `WASD` or arrow keys
- Strike: `Space`, `J`, or left click
- Dash: `Shift` — briefly avoids damage
- Prana Pulse: `E` — a short-range area attack with a six-second cooldown
- Restart after victory: `R`

Defeat five Ashen Shades, collect their prana, then defeat Kaalvan, the Grove Warden. The shrine revives the player automatically after defeat.

## Assets and attribution
